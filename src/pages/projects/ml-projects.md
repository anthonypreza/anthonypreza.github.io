## Overview

This early collection of ML projects includes sentiment classification, session-based recommendations, click-through rate prediction, learning to rank, and an online/offline
feature-store experiment.

My goal here was just to get a bit deeper into how modern ML systems work. I won't explain each project in detail here, but feel free to browse the repository if you're interested.
